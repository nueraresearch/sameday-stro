Sameday STRO — Investor Memo (Launch Market: Monte Vista ±50 Miles)
Short‑Term Rental Operations Platform for Rural STR Corridors
1. Overview
Sameday STRO is an operations platform for short‑term rental hosts in rural, high‑variance markets. The platform provides:

Guaranteed same‑day turnover completion (for members, within defined SLA windows)

Priority routing based on membership tier

Modular consumable refill kits

One‑vendor cleaning + consumables

Calendar‑synced automation

Zone‑based routing for rural geography

The wedge:
Rural STR hosts have no reliable operations vendor.  
Cleaner scarcity + long travel times + seasonal spikes make same‑day readiness extremely difficult.
Sameday STRO solves this with a membership‑based SLA and priority routing engine.
