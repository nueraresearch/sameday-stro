# Cleaner Payout Shares for All Modifiers & Add-Ons

**Status:** final rule set provided by user

Cleaner payout is always:

**Base Pay + Mileage Pay + Modifier Shares + Add-On Shares + Bonuses (capped by tenure)**

Below are the modifier and add-on shares.

## Bathrooms — Cleaner Share
Bathrooms drive scrubbing time, so cleaners get a fixed payout per extra bathroom.

| Bathrooms | Host Add-On | Cleaner Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Included | Included |
| 2 | +$10 | $6 |
| 3 | +$20 | $12 |
| 4 | +$30 | $18 |
| 5+ | +$40 | $24 |

Cleaner share = **60%** of bathroom modifier.

## Beds — Cleaner Share
Beds drive linen load, so cleaners get a fixed payout per extra bed.

| Beds | Host Add-On | Cleaner Share |

| 4 | +$20 | $12 |

## Why These Shares Were Chosen
- Cleaner labor is compensated fairly.
- Most modifiers are labor-dominant, so a 60–67% cleaner share is the stated target.
- Same-day balances cleaner disruption, dispatch overhead, and margin stability.
- Mileage remains separate from these add-on shares.
