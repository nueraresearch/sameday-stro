🧩 Section 1 — Marketplace Principles
Sameday STRO operates on five core principles:

1. SLA Reliability
Turnovers must finish before check‑in.
All routing, pricing, and governance decisions protect SLA windows.

2. Cleaner Safety & Fairness
Cleaners must be protected from unsafe, abusive, or high‑risk hosts.
No cleaner is ever routed into danger.

3. Host Fairness & Transparency
Hosts must receive predictable pricing, routing, and SLA outcomes.
No host is ever penalized without clear cause.

4. Margin Stability
The marketplace must remain economically viable.
Bonus caps, risk surcharges, and routing rules protect margins.

5. Operational Predictability
The system must behave consistently across all markets.
Governance rules prevent chaos and ensure trust.

🧩 Section 2 — Participant Roles
Hosts
Provide properties, pay for turnovers, maintain listing accuracy, and follow check‑out rules.

Cleaners
Perform turnovers, maintain reliability, follow SLA windows, and upload QA photos.

Dispatch
Oversees routing, enforces governance rules, handles escalations, and protects SLA windows.

Platform
Provides pricing, routing, risk scoring, payouts, QA, and governance enforcement.
